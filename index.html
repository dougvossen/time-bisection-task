Timing Task

In this task, you will see a white circle appear briefly in the center of a dark box and then disappear. Your job is to decide how long the circle was visible.

You do not need to estimate the exact number of seconds. Instead, you will compare what you saw to two reference durations: a Short duration and a Long duration. Your job is to decide which one the circle felt closer to.

After each circle disappears, press S or click Short if it felt closer to the short duration. Press L or click Long if it felt closer to the long duration.

We will start with a short practice round. You will see clear examples of both the Short and the Long reference durations, with feedback after each one.

There are no trick questions. Please respond based on your honest first impression and try not to overthink it.

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can. When you are ready to begin the practice round, click the button below.
